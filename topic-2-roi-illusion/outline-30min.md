# Topic 2 — 30-Minute Run of Show

Axis: value measurement (is the investment working), not cost discipline — deliberately distinct from Topic 1's Inform/Optimize/Operate structure.

| Time | Headline | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–3:00 | *"40% of Agentic AI Projects Won't Survive 2027"* | Cold open on the real Gartner stat — not because the tech failed, because nobody could prove the value. |
| 3:00–7:00 | *"You're Asking the Wrong Question"* | Why "what's our ROI on AI" is malformed — cost and value measured in mismatched units. Reframe: are you even measuring the right thing yet. |
| 7:00–11:00 | *"Cost-Per-Prompt Is Lying to You — Cost-Per-Completed-Task Isn't"* | Cockroach Labs' reframe: agentic workflows burn 5-30x more tokens than simple chat. Track value-per-1,000-tokens instead. |
| 11:00–15:00 | *"Visibility Before Optimization — the Rule Everyone Skips"* | FinOps Foundation's real sequencing guidance: visibility and value-alignment before optimization. Most failures tried to fix cost before they could see it. |
| 15:00–19:00 | *"The Silence That Should Worry You"* | Salesforce, Workday, ServiceNow all report AI revenue prominently; none discloses AI cost growth. The silence is itself the talking point. |
| 19:00–24:00 | *"The Three Questions That Separate Signal From Theater"* | Concrete takeaway framework: (1) cost-per-completed-task vs. cost-per-call? (2) value-per-1,000-tokens trending up or down as usage grows? (3) could you defend this spend to a CFO with your own numbers? |
| 24:00–27:00 | *"A Real One: What Survived and What Got Cut"* | Worked example walking one investment through the three questions — a "survives" and a "gets cut" outcome side by side. **Needs a real, sourced case before delivery — not yet built.** |
| 27:00–30:00 | *"One Question for Founders, One Question for Investors"* | Close, parallel to Topic 1: founders — can you answer the three questions right now; investors — ask a startup these three before the pitch deck. CTA. |
