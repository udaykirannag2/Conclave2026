In this talk, [Name] — a FinOps and AI cost-governance practitioner who has personally negotiated $150M+ in enterprise cloud commitments and built real, working AI cost-attribution infrastructure — breaks down why "what's our ROI on AI" is often the wrong question, and what actually separates AI investments that survive their first budget review from the 40% that don't.
